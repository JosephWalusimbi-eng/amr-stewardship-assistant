DOSING GUIDANCE 
50. Dosing guidance - Children
Table 50.1 lists the antibiotics and suggested doses to be used in children for the conditions 
covered in the AWaRe book.
For children, weight-based dosing is used for oral treatment when possible. The 
2019 EML report on consensus guidance on paediatric antibiotic dosing regimens was used 
as a reference but adapted and weight-banded by infection and severity of disease (20). 
Local and national dosing guidance should be considered where available.
